About

Software that earns its place in your business.

We're a small software team based in Toronto that builds fast, custom websites and business software for small companies — automating the everyday work, like stock, sales and bookings, along the way.

You don't need a technical plan. Describe how the business works, in English or Amharic, and we'll turn it into something that runs.

Every project is built for your business alone. No templates, no outsourcing — you work directly with the team building it, start to finish.

We take on a limited number of projects at a time, so every client gets direct communication, careful attention, and a high-quality result.
