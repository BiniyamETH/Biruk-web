Questions

Asked before every project.

I don't have photos or text. Can you still build it?

Yes. Most owners start with nothing written down. Send phone photos of the shop and the products, tell me the prices and hours in a voice note if that's easier, and I'll write the wording and edit the photos. You approve everything before it goes live.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, what you sell or do, prices if you want them shown, opening hours, address, phone number, and any photos you have. That's enough for a first version.

How much does hosting cost after launch?

A domain is roughly $15–20 a year. Hosting for a site like yours is usually free or a few dollars a month, and I'll set it up in your own account so the bills and the control stay with you.

Can I update prices myself?

For a catalogue, yes — I can build a simple editor so you change prices and stock without touching code. For a small site, sending me the changes is faster and cheaper than learning an admin panel.

Why not Wix, Shopify or Squarespace?

They're fine for a basic page. They get slow, they charge monthly forever, and you can't move the site elsewhere. A hand-coded site loads faster, costs almost nothing to run, and belongs to you. If a builder genuinely fits your case better, I'll tell you.

What if I don't like it?

You see the first version within a few days, before the second payment. Revisions are included until you approve it. If we can't get there, you keep the deposit's worth of work and we stop.
